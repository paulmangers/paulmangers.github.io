About

I am a second year PhD student at the Statistics Department at the London School of Economics where I am part of the Probability in Finance and Insurance Group. I work under the supervision of Prof. Kostas Kardaras and Dr. David Itkin. Previously, I completed my bachelor's and my master's degree in Mathematics at ETH Zurich.

My research evolves around Stochastic Portfolio Theory, market frictions (e.g. price impact) and robust finance (how to incorporate model uncertainty). I am always open to possible collaborations, so do not hesitate to get in touch!
